This page explains how the current AdenClean preview works. It is not a final legal privacy notice or service contract; have the policies reviewed before public or commercial launch.

Privacy in this preview

What you may submit

  • Customers: name, email, town or postal code, requested service, preferred date and time, and optional notes.
  • Cleaning professionals: name, email, town or postal code, services offered, and an availability date and time window.
  • After a completed service: a 1–5 rating, review text, or a service-issue report.

How information is handled

Storage depends on how this preview is configured. In Appwrite mode, used for production, the AdenClean server sends submissions to the configured Appwrite database for storage. In local SQLite mode, submissions stay in a database file on the machine running the app; that mode is for development and tests only. Do not submit real customer or cleaner information in local SQLite mode.

Submissions are available to authorized administrators in the AdenClean admin area, where each administrator signs in with an individual account. Reviews and issue reports are private; they are not displayed as public testimonials. This preview does not send email updates.

There is no automatic deletion schedule or self-service tool to edit or delete a submission. Preview information may remain until an administrator removes it or resets the preview database. For questions about the preview or a submission, contact AdenClean at applications@adeneats.com or (240) 342-6088.

Keep sensitive details out

Use a town or postal code only. Do not submit street addresses, entry or alarm codes, payment or bank details, or other sensitive information. This preview does not process payments.

Service and availability terms

Service area: Silver Spring, Maryland.

  • A cleaning request is an inquiry, not a confirmed booking. An admin approval means ready for follow-up; it does not reserve a date, set a price, or assign a cleaner.
  • Cleaner availability is an expression of interest, not a job offer, employment agreement, or confirmed shift.
  • Wait for direct confirmation from AdenClean before relying on a service date or shift. The preview does not currently send confirmation messages.
  • Prices, payment arrangements, cancellations, refunds, and service guarantees are not established by this preview. Confirm those details directly before agreeing to a service.

Reviews and service issues

After a request is marked complete, the private feedback link can be used to submit one review and one service-issue report for that request. Feedback is reviewed internally and is not posted publicly. You can revisit the link to check an issue’s status; status changes do not trigger notifications.

The link does not currently expire. Anyone who has it may submit feedback for that request, so keep it private and share it only with the intended person. The preview does not promise a particular resolution, refund, or compensation for a reported issue.

Before using this for real services

This preview has no connected customer messaging, confirmed booking or payment flow, automatic data-retention schedule, or self-service deletion. Do not treat a preview submission as a live service arrangement.
