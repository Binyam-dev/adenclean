Privacy &
service policies.

This page explains what the AdenClean website and mobile app collect, how that information is used, and what to expect from a request. It applies to both.

Privacy

What we collect

  • Customers: name, email, town or postal code, requested service, preferred date and time, and optional notes.
  • Cleaning professionals: name, email, town or postal code, services offered, and an availability date and time window.
  • After a completed service: a 1–5 rating, review text, or a service-issue report.

We only collect what you type into a form. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone, and it has no advertising or analytics tools. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

We use your information to review your request or availability, follow up with you about it, and review feedback and issues. Reviews and issue reports are private; they are not displayed as public testimonials. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Where it is stored and who can see it

The live service stores submissions in a hosted Appwrite database and handles them with our server, which runs with a cloud hosting provider. These providers process data for us to run AdenClean. Only AdenClean administrators, each with an individual sign-in, can view submissions. Server logs may include your IP address for security and to limit abuse.

On your phone

After you send a request, the app saves a private link code for it in your phone’s secure storage so you can check its status and leave feedback later. Anyone with that code can view that request’s status and submit feedback for it. You can remove saved requests from your phone at any time under More, then Remove saved requests, or by deleting the app.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep submissions until you ask us to delete them; there is no automatic deletion schedule yet. To see, correct, or delete your information, contact AdenClean at applications@adeneats.com or (240) 342-6088 and include the reference shown after you submitted your request. We delete it by hand, so please allow some time.

Keep sensitive details out

Use a town or postal code only. Do not submit street addresses, entry or alarm codes, payment or bank details, or other sensitive information. AdenClean does not process payments.

Children

AdenClean is for adults arranging home cleaning. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this page, the date at the top changes too.

Service and availability terms

Service area: Silver Spring, Maryland.

  • A cleaning request is an inquiry, not a confirmed booking. An admin approval means ready for follow-up; it does not reserve a date, set a price, or assign a cleaner.
  • Cleaner availability is an expression of interest, not a job offer, employment agreement, or confirmed shift.
  • Wait for direct confirmation from AdenClean before relying on a service date or shift. The app and website do not send confirmation messages.
  • Prices, payment arrangements, cancellations, refunds, and service guarantees are not set by a request. Confirm those details directly before agreeing to a service.

Conduct, reports and blocking

Do not post abusive, harassing, threatening, unsafe, misleading or spam content in job notes, offers or names, and do not share phone numbers, email addresses or links in them. AdenClean may remove content and may suspend or remove accounts that break these rules.

  • In the app you can report a job or an offer, and block the other person. They are not told who reported them or that they were blocked.
  • We review every report within 24 hours. Contact: applications@adeneats.com.
  • Text is checked automatically before it is saved. The check is not perfect, so reports matter.
  • Blocking hides you from each other. A booking you already have together carries on until it is finished.

Reviews and service issues

After a request is marked complete, you can submit one review and one service-issue report for that request, in the app or through the private feedback link. Feedback is reviewed internally and is not posted publicly. You can come back to check an issue’s status; status changes do not trigger notifications.

The link does not currently expire. Anyone who has it may submit feedback for that request, so keep it private and share it only with the intended person. AdenClean does not promise a particular resolution, refund, or compensation for a reported issue.

What AdenClean does not do yet

There is no in-app messaging, confirmed booking, or payment, and no self-service deletion. Do not treat a submission as a confirmed service arrangement.
